      The city changes key

      When the sky
      gives the stage away

      Some evenings gather under a bridge. Others begin behind an unassuming door. In Austin, darkness is less an ending than a change of soundtrack.

      1. 01Watch the bridge

        Congress Avenue's bats are a seasonal possibility, not a nightly promise. Check local guidance before making an evening of it.

      2. 02Listen first

        Look beyond a nightlife district. Pick a performance for the music, check the door policy, and leave time to actually stay.

      3. 03Make a way home

        Check your return route before the last set. If you drive out of town, sunset does not extend park access.
